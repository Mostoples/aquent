5. Hak Anda Sebagai Pengguna5. Your Rights as a User

Sesuai UU PDP Pasal 5–11 dan GDPR Bab III, Anda berhak:

  • Akses — lihat data di Pengaturan Akun
  • Portabilitas — unduh data JSON via tombol "Export"
  • Koreksi — edit nama, foto, preferensi
  • Penghapusan — hapus akun permanen dari Zona Berbahaya
  • Penolakan — XAI menjelaskan setiap keputusan AI

Permohonan diproses dalam maksimal 30 hari kerja.

Under Indonesian PDP Law Art. 5–11 and GDPR Chapter III, you have the right to:

  • Access — view your data in Account Settings
  • Portability — download JSON data via the "Export" button
  • Rectification — edit name, photo, preferences
  • Erasure — permanently delete your account from the Danger Zone
  • Objection — XAI explains every AI decision

Requests are processed within 30 business days.

6. Penyimpanan & Retensi6. Storage & Retention
