MemulaiGetting Started

Apa itu AQUENT?What is AQUENT?

AQUENT adalah platform smart shower berbasis IoT + AI yang memantau kualitas air mandi (pH, suhu, kekeruhan) secara real-time, dan memberikan rekomendasi kesehatan kulit personal. Semua keputusan AI dilengkapi penjelasan transparan (XAI).

AQUENT is an IoT + AI smart shower platform that monitors bathing water quality (pH, temperature, turbidity) in real time and provides personalized skin-health recommendations. Every AI decision comes with transparent explanations (XAI).

Bagaimana cara mendaftar?How do I sign up?

Buka halaman login, klik tab Daftar, lalu pilih Google atau email + password (minimal 6 karakter). Akun baru otomatis dapat paket Free.

Open the login page, click the Sign Up tab, then choose Google or email + password (minimum 6 characters). New accounts get the Free plan automatically.

Saya butuh hardware apa?What hardware do I need?

Untuk fitur dasar (dashboard, AI Chat, skin scan, ensiklopedia), tidak butuh hardware. Untuk monitoring real butuh ESP32 + sensor pH, suhu, turbidity yang push data ke Firebase.

For basic features (dashboard, AI Chat, skin scan, encyclopedia), no hardware is needed. For real monitoring you need an ESP32 + pH, temperature, and turbidity sensors that push data to Firebase.

FiturFeatures

Apa itu Quality Score 0-100?What is the 0-100 Quality Score?

Ringkasan kualitas air dari 3 parameter: pH (35%, ideal 6.5-7.5), Suhu (35%, ideal 36-38°C), Kekeruhan (30%, ideal ≤0.5 NTU). Grade A (90-100) hingga F (<45). Klik "Kenapa skor ini?" untuk penjelasan XAI.

A water quality summary from 3 parameters: pH (35%, ideal 6.5-7.5), Temperature (35%, ideal 36-38°C), Turbidity (30%, ideal ≤0.5 NTU). Grade A (90-100) to F (<45). Click "Why this score?" for the XAI explanation.

Bagaimana Skin Scanner bekerja tanpa Gemini?How does the Skin Scanner work without Gemini?

Skin Scanner AQUENT 100% lokal di browser Anda — foto tidak pernah dikirim ke server. Pipeline: TensorFlow.js model (klasifikasi tipe kulit) + pixel analysis (hidrasi, kemerahan, oiliness, tekstur). Bisa jalan offline setelah model di-cache.

AQUENT's Skin Scanner runs 100% locally in your browser — photos are never sent to a server. Pipeline: TensorFlow.js model (skin type classification) + pixel analysis (hydration, redness, oiliness, texture). Works offline once the model is cached.

Bagaimana mengganti tema & bahasa?How do I change theme & language?

Klik ikon tema (matahari/bulan/sparkle) di header untuk 3 mode: Dark, Light, Elegant. Klik tombol ID/EN untuk ganti bahasa.

Click the theme icon (sun/moon/sparkle) in the header for 3 modes: Dark, Light, Elegant. Click the ID/EN button to switch language.

Privasi & KeamananPrivacy & Security

Apakah foto skin scan saya disimpan?Are my skin scan photos stored?

Tidak. Foto wajah diproses sepenuhnya di browser via TensorFlow.js dan tidak pernah dikirim ke server. Hanya hasil analisis (teks) yang tersimpan.

No. Facial photos are processed entirely in your browser via TensorFlow.js and never sent to a server. Only the analysis results (text) are saved.

Bagaimana cara hapus akun & download data?How do I delete my account & download my data?

Buka Pengaturan Akun. Untuk hapus: bagian Zona Berbahaya. Untuk download: bagian Privasi & Data → Export (file JSON). Sesuai UU PDP & GDPR.

Open Account Settings. To delete: the Danger Zone section. To download: the Privacy & Data section → Export (JSON file). Compliant with Indonesian PDP Law & GDPR.

Sensor menampilkan "Demo Mode"Sensors show "Demo Mode"

Device IoT belum push data ke Firebase. Cek ESP32 terhubung WiFi, config Firebase benar, dan rules mengizinkan write. Demo Mode tetap menampilkan simulasi data realistis.

The IoT device hasn't pushed data to Firebase yet. Check that the ESP32 is connected to WiFi, the Firebase config is correct, and rules allow writes. Demo Mode still shows realistic simulated data.

Skin scanner gagal akses kameraSkin scanner can't access camera

Browser butuh izin kamera & akses via https://. Klik "Allow" saat diminta. Alternatif: klik upload foto dari galeri.

The browser needs camera permission & access via https://. Click "Allow" when prompted. Alternative: click upload a photo from gallery.
