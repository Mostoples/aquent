02 · HOW IT WORKS

The same water,
used again.

AQUENT mounts on the bathroom wall. Used shower water isn't drained away — it's cleaned and sent back to the shower.

1

Greywater collected

Used shower water flows into the floor drain, then is pumped up into AQUENT.

2

6-layer filtration

Bamboo leaf, loofah, zeolite, banana-peel biochar, chitosan, sugarcane bagasse.

3

UV-C sterilization

UV-C light at 254 nm deactivates any remaining bacteria and viruses.

4

4-sensor check

pH, turbidity, residual chlorine (ORP), and temperature are checked in real time.

5

AI decides

Passes the test → reused. Fails → drained. An integrated heater prepares warm water.

6

Back to the shower

Clean water and warm water meet at the mixer — ready for the next shower.

03 · THE APP

Everything in view,
right in your hand.

Smart monitoring

Water quality score, four sensors, and filter status refresh every 2 seconds.

Shower control

Set the temperature, choose room-temperature or warm water, and track water used vs. recycled.

Tropical & subtropical shower schedule

Tropical: 2–3× a day with room-temperature water; subtropical: 1–2× a day, adjusting to the season.

AI Dermatology

Scan your skin, analyze moisture and sensitivity, then get shower advice that matches your water quality.

04 · IMPACT

Save clean water every day.

Treatment right at the point of use, with no separate tank. Water-saving showers cut use by up to 20–50%, and AQUENT reuses water that would otherwise be drained.
