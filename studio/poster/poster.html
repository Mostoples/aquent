BACKGROUND

1.4 Millionpeople die every year

Due to poor access to sanitation, water and hygiene — mainly in low- and middle-income countries.

4 Billiontwo-thirds of the world

Face water scarcity; 500 million year-round, mainly in India and China.

3 Billionaffected by 2050

Severe water-scarce river basins may triple due to pollution.

30% → 40%with water quality factor

Scarcity rises when quality is counted; 55% of the world faces severe scarcity.

UNESCO / UN-Water 2023 · Mekonnen & Hoekstra 2016 · van Vliet et al. 2021–22 · Nature Communications 2024
2

PROBLEM

60–80 L per shower, discarded

Used once and sent to the drain, though greywater is far less polluted than blackwater.

Groundwater drying out

Jakarta runs a 4,000 L/s deficit; major rivers lost over 40% of their flow in 2025.

Unknown water quality

Users cannot tell whether bathing water is safe for their skin.

3

SOLUTIONS

AQUENT sanitises greywater through natural multilayer filtration and returns it to the shower head — verified in real time by four sensors, paired with an AI dermatology assistant.

HOW TO USE

1
Install once

Wall-mounted above the shower: mains, return line, power, WiFi.

2
Open the app

Check water score, filter health and litres recycled.

3
Set temperature & Eco

Room temp or warm 37–40 °C; Eco reuses treated water.

4
Shower as usual

Greywater is filtered, sterilised and checked automatically.

5
Watch it live

pH, turbidity, chlorine and temperature in real time.

6
After the shower

Derma AI skin scan; schedule & filter reminders.

Maintenance · rinse the sediment screen weekly · new cartridge ≈ every 1,500 L

SMART APPLICATION

Live water quality

pH, turbidity, residual chlorine and temperature streamed in real time.

Smart scheduling

Tropical 2–3×/day at 20–25 °C; subtropical 1–2×/day, 37–40 °C when cold.

Skin score & XAI

Every recommendation carries per-factor attribution and a confidence score.
