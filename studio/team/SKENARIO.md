## Catatan audio
- Suara bicara diberi noise reduction ringan, EQ high-pass 80 Hz, dan loudness -16 LUFS.
- Musik turun -14 dB saat ada dialog.

## Teks yang perlu dicek tim (hasil transkripsi kurang jelas)
1. **Kinan:** "I act as the *[legendary business girl?]*, managing brand identity…" Frasa ini tidak terdengar jelas.
2. **Klip iklim:** "…longest in the last two decades due to the *[unknown phenomena / El Niño phenomenon?]*"
3. **Dhafa:** diucapkan "3.6 billion **percent**", di subtitle ditulis "3.6 billion **people**, or 46 percent".
4. **Lais:** diucapkan "obtained by a single **pet**", di subtitle ditulis "used for a single **bath**".
